## 1. Market Research & Sizing (T001)

### Overall Beer Market
- US beer shipments 2025: ~183-185 million barrels (-5% YoY, lowest since mid-1980s).
- Retail dollar sales: Estimates range $110B-$125B (mixed sources; multi-outlet scans lower). Forecasts value growth to $140B+ by 2030 via premiumization and NA.
- Craft production: ~$7.4B in 2026 (stagnant, CAGR ~1.8% past 5yrs). Volume share ~13%.
- Packaging: Aluminum cans ~63% of volume.

### Trends & Opportunity
Category under pressure (volume declines, moderation, competition from NA/THC/seltzers). Innovation in packaging and experience can capture attention. Premium/super-premium and occasion-based drinking show relative strength.

### Shotgun Culture
- Niche but culturally embedded: college parties, tailgates (NFL/college football), festivals (Coachella annual pre-gate shotgun events draw 20-450+ participants), sports celebrations (e.g. post-Super Bowl, #ShotgunJake).
- Social media amplification, celebrity/athlete participation.
- Supporting products: Puncturing tools (Shotgun Shell, keychain tools, Pump Action) sell commercially (thousands of units reported in case studies).
- Addressable market proxy: Segment of 21-34 beer drinkers engaging in rapid-consumption games/occasions. Conservative estimate: low single-digit % of relevant occasions. Differentiated can could expand the ritual or make it safer/cleaner.

### Competitors / Prior Art
- No major commercial dual-opening beer can found in market.
- Patents: US20150183547A1 (shotgun accessible can with side score), WO2022006250A1 (dual mouth shotgun can), other secondary vent designs. Freedom-to-operate analysis required.
- Adjacent: Shotgun Hard Seltzer (existed, discontinued), reusable "Shotgun Bottle" products, various tools.

### Target Demo
Primary: 21-35, party/tailgate/sports culture, male-leaning but inclusive. Secondary: novelty gift/occasion buyers.

## 2. Manufacturing & Legal Feasibility (T002)

### Cost Estimates
- Standard 12oz can body + lid + basic print: $0.12-$0.25 landed per unit (volume dependent; lower at 100k+).
- Digital print: Low MOQ (~1.5k-7k), higher unit cost.
- Offset: High MOQ (50k-200k+), lower unit.
- Custom side secondary opening (scored panel + tab or similar): Significant NRE tooling/dies from can maker (Ball, Crown, etc.). Estimate $20k-$100k+ for development/testing (precise residual thickness, integrity under pressure ~30 psi, transport, shelf life). Per-unit premium initially high until amortized.
- Co-packing/filling: Starts ~$4k-$10k, MOQ thousands of liters/cans.
- Pilot path: Concept prototypes (mock/3D/modified cans) → small digital run of standard cans + marketing of "tool-friendly" or partner for true dual → scale.

### Safety & Technical
- Side wall scoring must maintain pressure integrity, resist accidental opening, produce clean edges (no jagged aluminum injury risk).
- User flow: Open side first (mouth seal), then top tab for vent/flow.
- Testing required: Pressure, drop, seal longevity, flow rate, foam control.
- Prefer light lager (~4-5% ABV) for lower foam/alcohol load.

### Legal / Regulatory
- TTB: Certificate of Label Approval (COLA) mandatory for malt beverages in interstate commerce (bottler obtains). Mandatory statements, no misleading claims. Novel packaging OK if labels accurate.
- FDA: Food-contact materials, container closure integrity (esp. pressurized).
- State three-tier distribution system applies for full commercial beer sales.
- Liability: High for promoting rapid alcohol intake (BAC spike, choking, injury). Mitigations: Age-gate (21+), "drink responsibly" prominent, safety instructions, product liability insurance, focus on controlled/lite product.
- Patent risk: Design around prior art or license/search FTO.

**Overall Feasibility Flag:** Medium-High risk/cost for true integrated side-opening at launch. Recommend phased: Brand + standard can + companion tool or "shotgun-ready" messaging first, while developing custom can.

1. Concept validation: Target demo interest survey ≥55-60% "would try/buy".
2. Mechanism: Prototype demonstrates reliable seal, safe open force, effective flow without excessive foam/spray.
3. Legal/IP: COLA path clear, FTO acceptable or design-around complete.
4. Unit economics model: Path to >40% gross margin at scale, CAC payback <6 months.
